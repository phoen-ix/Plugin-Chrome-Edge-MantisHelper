# Privacy Policy — Mantis Helper

Last updated: 2026-09-25

Mantis Helper does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it sends nothing to its developer or to anyone else.

- **Settings:** the only thing the extension saves is the Mantis URL you enter in its options. It is kept in your browser's extension storage (and synced by your browser if you use Chrome or Edge sync). It is never sent to the developer.
- **Opening issues:** when you choose "Open in Mantis", the extension opens the selected issue number as a link on the Mantis server you configured, like clicking a link. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
- **No tracking:** the extension contains no analytics, no tracking and no remote code.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/phoen-ix/Plugin-Chrome-Edge-MantisHelper/issues.
